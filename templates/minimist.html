Minimist Prototype Pollution Vulnerability

CVE-2021-44906

minimist before 1.2.6 (and <0.2.4) is vulnerable to prototype pollution. Parsing malicious argument strings can pollute the Object prototype, potentially leading to denial of service or other security impacts.

CVSS Score: 7.3 (High)

Vulnerability Type: Prototype Pollution

Fixed Version: 1.2.6+

Current Version: 1.2.0 (Vulnerable)
